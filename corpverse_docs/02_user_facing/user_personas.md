# User Personas

## Persona 1: The Job-Seeking Student ("Meet Priya")

- **Who:** Final-year engineering student, decent technical skills, weak on interview confidence and resume presentation.
- **Goals:** Wants realistic practice applying and interviewing without the stakes of a real rejection following her around.
- **Frustrations:** Doesn't know why she's getting rejected in real applications; existing mock-interview tools feel generic and don't reference her actual resume/domain.
- **How she uses CorpVerse:** Spends most time in the Job Seeker Dashboard, applies to multiple roles, reads every rejection feedback carefully, reapplies after "improving" her profile.

## Persona 2: The Working Player ("Meet Rohan")

- **Who:** Already comfortable with the platform, got hired at one of the seed companies, now focused on growing.
- **Goals:** Wants to see clear progress — EXP, promotions, raises — and understand what "good performance" looks like in a structured way.
- **Frustrations:** Wants promotion criteria to feel fair and legible, not like a black box.
- **How he uses CorpVerse:** Lives in the Employee Dashboard, checks tasks daily, tracks EXP progress toward next promotion, eventually accumulates enough to consider founder mode.

## Persona 3: The Aspiring Founder ("Meet Ananya")

- **Who:** A player who's built up enough EXP and wants to try running her own company instead of climbing someone else's ladder.
- **Goals:** Wants to experience what it's like to post roles, screen candidates, and manage a small team — even if no real players apply right away.
- **Frustrations:** Doesn't want her company to feel "dead" if nobody applies immediately.
- **How she uses CorpVerse:** Uses the Founder Dashboard, creates her company, posts roles, and relies on the mini-model hiring fallback to keep her company staffed until real players show interest.

## Persona 4 (Supporting, not primary): The Evaluator/Mentor

- **Who:** Not a regular platform user, but someone (like an external mentor or project evaluator) who needs to understand the system quickly during a demo.
- **Goals:** Wants to see the full loop (apply → hire → work → promote → found) work end-to-end without needing background explanation.
- **How this shapes the product:** Reinforces why a clean, demoable MVP path matters more than covering every edge case.
