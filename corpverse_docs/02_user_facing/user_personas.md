# User Personas

## Persona 1: The Job-Seeking Student ("Priya")

- **Background:** Final-year Computer Science & Engineering student (21 years old). Possesses solid coding fundamentals (Java, Python, basic Web Dev), but has received 0 job offers after 15 campus placement submissions.
- **Behavioral Patterns:** Spends hours reading interview questions online, gets overwhelmed by ATS resume rejection emails with zero feedback, lacks confidence during spoken/chat interview rounds.
- **Goals:**
  - Wants realistic, low-stakes practice for resume screening and technical interviews.
  - Wants to know *specifically why* a resume or interview response failed so she can fix it.
  - Wants to experience what happens *after* getting hired (job tasks, promotions, raises).
- **Frustrations:** "I keep getting template rejection emails saying 'we decided to move forward with other candidates'. I don't know if my resume formatted wrong or if my skills were missing."
- **How She Uses CorpVerse:** Registers via Clerk, uploads her resume, applies to NovaTech Solutions for a Junior Backend Developer position. After a screening rejection, she reads the specific feedback, updates her profile skills, waits out the 48h cooldown, and reapplies successfully.

## Persona 2: The Working Player ("Rohan")

- **Background:** Junior Developer on CorpVerse (23 years old). Has been on the platform for 2 weeks, works at GreenPulse Energy.
- **Behavioral Patterns:** Logs in daily to check assigned tasks, executes task items, tracks EXP gains toward the next promotion threshold.
- **Goals:**
  - Wants a legible, transparent career progression path (Junior → Mid → Senior).
  - Wants to accumulate enough EXP (500 EXP) to unlock Founder Mode.
  - Wants to understand performance reviews and promotion mechanics.
- **Frustrations:** In real-world jobs, promotion criteria feel like a black box controlled by corporate politics.
- **How He Uses CorpVerse:** Lives inside the Employee Dashboard. Completes tasks (+20 EXP each), tracks his progress bar toward 200 EXP for Mid-Level promotion, and monitors his path toward founding his own clean-tech firm.

---

## Persona 3: The Aspiring Founder ("Ananya")

- **Background:** Senior Employee on CorpVerse who just hit 500 EXP (22 years old). Ambitious, interested in startup dynamics and product management.
- **Behavioral Patterns:** Reads tech startup blogs, wants to experience posting job roles, evaluating real applicant resumes, and building a team.
- **Goals:**
  - Wants to experience founding a company, defining company brand/domain, and creating job roles.
  - Wants to review candidate applications through the platform screening engine.
- **Frustrations:** Has no capital or real-world experience to launch a real startup yet, but wants practice managing hiring pipelines.
- **How She Uses CorpVerse:** Resigns from her Senior role, unlocks Founder Dashboard, names her company "Ananya Tech Labs", posts 2 developer roles, and reviews real player applications.

## Persona 4: The Evaluator / Academic Mentor ("Dr. Mehta")

- **Background:** Senior Professor / SGP Minor Project Evaluator.
- **Goals:**
  - Wants to verify technical depth (Node.js REST API, MongoDB Atlas schemas, Clerk auth, AI microservice).
  - Wants to test the full lifecycle loop end-to-end within 5 minutes during a live demo.
- **Impact on Product:** Drives the need for a clean landing page, fast loading times, demo seed data (5 companies), and robust error handling.
